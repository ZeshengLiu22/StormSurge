Preempt behavior and limits
preempt is an interruptible queue using otherwise idle resources; it does not
promise 20 uninterrupted minutes or a quicker start. Existing #PBS -r n is
preserved: a preempted job is not automatically rerun. train.sh stops on SIGTERM;
these launchers do not resume a prior checkpoint. Any manual retry creates a
fresh timestamped run. Live Derecho GPU execution and scheduling were not tested.
